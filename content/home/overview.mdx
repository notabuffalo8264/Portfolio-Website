title: "About Me"
---

Hi, I’m Christopher. I’m a senior at Michigan State University studying Mechanical Engineering, and I’m planning to continue on for a master’s in Mechanical Engineering after graduation.

I’ve always been the kind of person who learns best by building things. Sometimes that means designing and fabricating something physical, and other times it means spending an entire weekend building an app, automating a workflow, or figuring out a problem simply because I thought it would be interesting. My projects have taken me through mechanical design, electronics, materials research, software development, AI, and plenty of areas I knew almost nothing about when I started.

That process of learning something new is probably my favorite part. When a project grabs my attention, I tend to dive into it completely, researching, experimenting, refining, and occasionally spending far too much time perfecting the workflow before I even start the actual project. I especially enjoy finding ways to use AI as a tool for learning and building. I think its biggest potential is not simply doing work for us, but helping us learn faster, explore ideas more effectively, and accomplish things that would otherwise be out of reach as an individual.

I also really enjoy helping other people learn. I’ve spent time tutoring and teaching both friends and other students, and I like taking something complicated and finding a way to make it click.

Outside of engineering, you’ll usually find me playing tennis, rock climbing, skiing, trading and investing, or working on whatever personal project has most recently taken over my free time. I also love making physical things and want to get much deeper into woodworking once I have a workshop of my own.  The first major project on my list is building my own desk.

Long term, I want to work on problems that are genuinely difficult and constantly evolving. I’m most excited by environments where there isn’t an obvious answer, where technology is being pushed forward, and where I’m expected to keep learning. I don’t want to spend my career solving the same problem over and over again. I want to be surrounded by interesting people, ambitious ideas, and challenges that make me leave work knowing something I didn’t know that morning.
